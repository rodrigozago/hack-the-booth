# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on learning and collaboration.
- Share knowledge openly.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Disruptive or exclusionary behavior

## Scope

This applies to project spaces, workshops, events, and online channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or ban repeat offenders when necessary to protect the community.
